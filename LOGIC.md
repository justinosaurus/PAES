# CICS Student Admission System

**Technology:** Laravel Blade and PHP

## Admission Process

1. **Create a student account**  
   Students register or log in and complete a form with their personal information, student status (freshman, transferee, shifter, or irregular), contact details, and other required information.

2. **Upload admission documents**  
   Students submit their admission test results and other requirements.

3. **Schedule the CICS exam**  
   Students book an appointment for the CICS exam. Each day has a limit of 50 student slots.

4. **Receive appointment details**  
   The system emails students a printable copy containing their appointment stamp, reference number, requirements, and reminders.

5. **Open the dashboard**  
   After scheduling, students are redirected to their account dashboard.

6. **Check the exam result**  
   Once exam results are released, students can log in to see whether they passed or failed. Accounts belonging to students who fail will be disposed of after 30 days.

7. **Schedule an interview and submit requirements**  
   Students who pass schedule an interview appointment and submit the required documents.

8. **Receive an admission status update**  
   The system notifies students whether they have passed the interview and been approved.

9. **Pay department and other required fees**  
   Approved students are assigned to batches of two or three for payment.

## Student Account Features

- Dashboard
- Personal information
- Admission status
- Requirements checklist
- Announcements and news feed
- Information about CICS history and courses
